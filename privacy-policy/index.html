Privacy Policy for Roll

Last updated: March 20, 2026

Syntax Games ("we", "us", or "our") developed and operates Roll ("the Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Roll.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Roll uses third-party services to provide Sign in with Google, save and restore game progress in the cloud, optional Google Play Games features, and advertisements.

The information processed through these services may depend on the features you use, your device, your account settings, and the configuration of the services.

2. Sign-In, Cloud Save, and Google Play Games

Sign in with Google is required to use Roll. Sign-in is provided through Google and Unity Gaming Services (Authentication). Roll does not create a separate Syntax Games account.

When you are signed in, game progress (such as campaign levels, stars, Roll Coins, skins, and related settings) is stored and restored using Unity Gaming Services (Cloud Save), linked to your signed-in account.

This may include information such as:

  • Game progress and unlock state
  • Roll Coins and other in-game progress
  • Game state necessary to restore progress
  • Audio and haptics preferences stored in your save

Google Play Games Services is optional. Roll may use it for features such as achievements where enabled on your device. Achievement progress is stored in your Google Play Games profile when you use those features. You can play Roll without using Play Games achievements.

Google and Unity may process information associated with your Google account, Unity player identity, and device in order to provide these services.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms. Unity's handling of information is governed by Unity's applicable privacy policies and terms.

For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy and Unity's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising

Roll displays advertisements using Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect and process information from your device and information about your interaction with advertisements. Depending on the applicable advertising configuration, consent settings, device settings, and applicable laws, this may include information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements.

In some regions, a consent message may be shown before personalized advertising, in line with applicable law and your choices.

Google AdMob is provided by Google. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, please visit:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services

4. Haptic Feedback

Roll may use haptic feedback on supported devices to provide gameplay feedback.

Haptic feedback itself does not require us to collect personal information.

5. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

Syntax Games does not operate its own server or database for storing player information for Roll.

Roll does not intentionally collect information such as your contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or precise location for its core gameplay functionality.

However, third-party services integrated into Roll, including Google, Unity, and Google AdMob, may independently process information as described in their respective policies.

6. How Information Is Used

Information processed in connection with Roll may be used to:

  • Provide required Sign in with Google and sync game progress
  • Save and restore game progress across devices while you are signed in
  • Provide optional achievements and other Play Games features
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Maintain and improve the Game
  • Identify and address technical problems
  • Help prevent abuse, fraud, or misuse of the Game

7. Third-Party Services

Roll uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

These services include:

  • Unity Gaming Services — Authentication and Cloud Save for required sign-in and game progress.
  • Google Play Games Services — optional achievements and related Play Games features where enabled.
  • Google AdMob — advertisements within the Game.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

Google's Privacy Policy

8. Data Storage and Security

Game-related progress is stored using Unity Cloud Save for your signed-in account, so that it can be restored on another device using the same account.

Syntax Games does not operate its own server or database for storing this player information.

Information processed by third-party services is handled according to the security practices and policies of those services. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Roll does not create or maintain a separate Syntax Games player account.

You may reset campaign progress from within the Game (Settings → Reset Progress). That does not remove your Google sign-in, Roll Coins, unlocked skins, or optional Google Play Games achievements. For details, see our Delete your data page.

Cloud save data and sign-in are subject to Unity's and Google's applicable retention and management practices. Optional Google Play Games achievement data is subject to Google's Play Games policies.

Information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's applicable data retention and deletion practices.

For information about managing Google Play Games data, please visit:

Google's Play Games data management information

10. Children's Privacy

Roll is a general-audience game and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

The Game may nevertheless be accessible to users of different ages. We do not intentionally design Roll to collect personal information from children.

Third-party services used by Roll may have their own requirements and policies relating to users of different ages.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the features, services, or data practices of Roll change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
