Roll

By Syntax Games

Roll is a mobile puzzle game for Android. Guide a ball through campaign levels, earn stars, unlock hints, and collect Roll Coins and skins as you progress. Optional Sign in with Google saves your progress to the cloud so you can continue on another device. The game may show ads and supports optional in-app purchases through Google Play.
