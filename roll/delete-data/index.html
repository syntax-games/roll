Delete Your Data — Roll

Last updated: March 20, 2026

Syntax Games ("we", "us", or "our") developed and operates Roll ("the Game"). This page explains how you can reset campaign progress in the app and where related data is stored.

Sign in with Google is required to use Roll. When you are signed in, progress is saved using Unity Gaming Services (Cloud Save), linked to your account. Roll does not create a separate Syntax Games account. See our Privacy Policy for more detail.

1. Reset Game Progress (In the App)

Use this if you want to replay the campaign from the beginning. It does not remove your Google sign-in, Roll Coins, or unlocked skins.

Steps:

  • Open Roll
  • Go to Settings
  • Tap Reset Progress
  • Confirm the dialog

What Reset Progress clears:

  • Campaign level completion and unlock state
  • Best star ratings per level

What Reset Progress does not change:

  • Your Google sign-in
  • Roll Coins and skins you have unlocked
  • Per-level collected stage Roll Coins already saved in your game data
  • Google Play Games achievements (if enabled)

In-level help hints (which props to place) are handled during gameplay on your device and are not stored in your cloud save. Reset Progress does not change how hints work in a level session.

After a reset, your updated campaign state may be saved to the cloud when you play again while signed in.

2. Sign-In and Cloud Save

Reset Progress does not sign you out. To stop using cloud sync, you may sign out from the Game if that option is available, or remove the app from your device.

Deleting or managing data held by Google or Unity (including cloud save tied to your sign-in) may require using the account and privacy controls provided by those services, in addition to any options inside Roll.

3. Google Play Games Achievements (Optional)

If you use optional Google Play Games achievements in Roll, that progress is stored in your Google Play Games profile. Reset Progress in Roll does not remove it. Manage Play Games data using Google's account controls.

For more information, see Google's Play Games data management information .

4. Advertising Data

Roll may display ads through Google AdMob (or related Google advertising services). Advertising data is processed according to Google's policies and your device or consent settings. See Google's Privacy Policy.
